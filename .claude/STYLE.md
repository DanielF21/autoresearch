# Writing style for this repo

## Structure

Explain in this order. Do not skip the first step.

1. **Why this matters to the project.** Restate the goal and the specific risk being addressed.
2. **What question this was meant to answer.** State it as a question.
3. **What we ran.** The configurations, and why each one exists.
4. **What we found.** Numbered findings.
5. **What it means for the project.** The variables at stake, the number for each, and the response to each.

## Sentences

Short. Declarative. One idea per sentence.

Define every term the first time it appears. If a term is a Linux concept, an operating system counter, or a piece of project jargon, explain what it is before using it in a conclusion.

No metaphors. No analogies. "The warning light is broken" is wrong. "The counter did not register the slowdown" is right.

No punchy fragments. No sentences that land a point instead of stating a fact.

## Numbers

Give the number. Give what it was measured against. Give how many times it was observed.

Separate what is established from what is suspected. If direction is not established, say so.

## Failures

Report them first and plainly. Say what broke, what it would have caused, and what changed.

## Banned

Em dashes. Hyphens joining two words as punctuation.
